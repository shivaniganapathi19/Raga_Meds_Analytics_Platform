# Project Insights

## Executive KPIs

- Calculated total revenue and total profit generated by the business.
- Measured gross profit margin to evaluate overall profitability.
- Analyzed average order value and total orders placed.
- Calculated current inventory quantity and total inventory value.
- Identified medicines that require immediate reordering.

---

## Customer Insights

- Identified the most valuable customers based on lifetime spending.
- Analyzed repeat customers and customer purchase frequency.
- Compared customer spending across different cities.
- Evaluated customer purchasing behavior by age group.
- Ranked customers using lifetime value.
- Performed RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis for customer segmentation.

---

## Inventory Insights

- Monitored stock availability across all medicines.
- Identified low-stock and out-of-stock medicines.
- Calculated inventory value by medicine, warehouse, and category.
- Ranked medicines based on inventory value.
- Measured inventory turnover ratio.
- Tracked medicine expiry status and remaining shelf life.

---

## Sales Insights

- Analyzed monthly and daily sales performance.
- Measured month-over-month sales growth using the LAG() window function.
- Evaluated monthly profit trends.
- Identified the highest revenue-generating days.
- Compared payment method performance.
- Ranked the best-selling medicines by revenue and quantity.
- Measured revenue contribution of each medicine.
- Calculated profit margin for every medicine.
- Built running revenue calculations using window functions.
- Ranked monthly sales performance.
- Identified the top 5 medicines in each category.

---

## Purchase Insights

- Analyzed monthly purchase trends and growth.
- Calculated average purchase value.
- Identified the most purchased medicines.
- Compared purchase costs across medicines and categories.
- Built running purchase cost analysis.
- Ranked top purchased medicines within each category.

---

## Supplier Insights

- Evaluated supplier purchase value and purchase volume.
- Compared supplier contribution to total procurement.
- Ranked suppliers based on purchase value.
- Measured average purchase value by supplier.
- Counted distinct medicines supplied by each supplier.
- Calculated the average number of medicines purchased per order.
- Identified the highest-value purchase made from each supplier.

---

# Business Value

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw transactional healthcare data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making.

The analytics help management:

- Increase revenue
- Improve profitability
- Optimize inventory levels
- Monitor supplier performance
- Understand customer behavior
- Support data-driven business decisions
